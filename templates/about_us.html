About Propoert Connect

Who We Are

Property Connect is a modern property discovery platformdesigned to simplify the way people find homes, hostels, apartemnts, guesthouses and hotels. We focus on providing verified and reliable property listings to eliminate fraud and reduce the time spent searching for accommodation.

Our system is built with a strong commitment to transparency, trust and efficiency, ensuring that every user finds exactly what they are looking for.

Our Mission

To provide trusted and efficient platform where tenants can easily find verified properties without the risks associated wuth brokers and scams.

Our Vision

To become the leading property connection platform in Uganda, East Africa and beyond by delivering reliable and innovative housing solutions.

How We Work

Agent Verification

Our agents physically visit and verify every property before uploading it to the system.

Accurate Listings

Each property is listed with correct details, including type, price and location.

Direct Contact

Tenants can directly contact landlords after a small verification fee.
